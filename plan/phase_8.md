# Phase 8 — Post-event validation and pilot path

**Window:** after event; planning starts after 1 Nov 2026 · **Status:** Future; not authorization to deploy.

## Objective
Make a responsible research/pilot candidate only after review. A prize or synthetic benchmark does not authorize real-patient use.

## Workstreams
1. **Patient/clinician review:** oncology clinicians, pathology/radiology experts, patient advocates, interpreters, accessibility experts; assess comprehension, tone, traceability, anxiety, questions and workflow.
2. **Formal safety case:** intended use, misuse, hazard log, severity/likelihood, residual risk, false reassurance/alarm, omissions/contradictions, human oversight, monitoring and stop rules.
3. **Privacy/legal/security:** data-flow inventory, threat model, vendor terms/DPA/region/retention, relevant Indian/international privacy rules with counsel, penetration test, consent/deletion/incident plan.
4. **Translation/accessibility:** professional medical translation; comprehension and assistive-tech studies; regional dialect/script tests.
5. **Pilot design:** supervised shadow-mode first; outcomes (teach-back/comprehension, confidence, prepared questions, workload, anxiety/error signals); ethics/research review as required; inclusion/exclusion, escalation, stopping and analysis plan.

## Gate before any real patient use
Clinical/stakeholder approval; representative validation across reports and languages; privacy/legal/security/ethics approvals as applicable; consent and data agreements; trained support; monitoring and incident response; approved deployment. No real-data pilot until all required gates are signed off.
