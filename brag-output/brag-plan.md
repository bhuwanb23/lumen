# Lumen — 60s brag plan

## Angle
Before an oncology appointment, confusing report wording becomes a calm, source-linked briefing — exact quotes, verified claims, neutral questions. Safety is the product.

## Hook
“Before your appointment” → “A clearer conversation starts with understanding the report.”

## Highlights
1. Paste synthetic excerpt + dual acknowledgements → Explain excerpt
2. Verified claims with **View source** revealing exact pathology wording
3. Care-team questions + fail-closed refusal (“Scope safety is a feature”)

## Punchline
“Evidence before eloquence.” Confirm meaning with your care team. ◉ Lumen

## Tone
`polished` — calm, clinical-adjacent, premium; humble about medicine.

- Brand mark: `◉ Lumen` · pill: `Prototype · English · text only`
